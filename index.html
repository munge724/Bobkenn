Our story

We followed our hearts back to the Mara.

Before guiding guests across the sweeping grasslands of the Masai Mara, Ken and Daisy worked side by side in an international NGO focused on conservation and community development in East Africa. It was rewarding, important work—but something was missing.

They missed the wild: the dawn chorus, the thrill of spotting a lioness across golden grass, and the stillness of the savannah at sunset. So they founded BobKen Tours, a small, personal safari company rooted in a genuine love for the Mara and its wildlife.

☀️

Small team. Big heart.

Every guest is treated as a friend. Every game drive is planned with care. And every moment in the bush is shared with the same wonder as that first Mara sunrise.

The people behind your safari

Meet your guides

Two experts, one shared passion—and a personal welcome to the place they call home.

Ken Kishoyian

Mara-born guide & conservationist

Born into the Maasai community of the Mara region, Ken has spent a lifetime learning the rhythms of the savannah. He reads animal behaviour, tracks wildlife, and spots the leopard others might miss.

His cultural roots open doors to authentic Maasai experiences. With years of conservation fieldwork, he brings both scientific knowledge and a deep connection to the land.

Daisy

Community development & conservation

Daisy’s background in community development and conservation biology gives her an extraordinary eye for the ecological stories unfolding around every game drive. She loves sharing the birds, plants, and smaller wonders of the Mara.

Her storytelling can turn a wildlife sighting into a lifelong memory. Daisy also makes sure every guest feels at home, from the first message to the last farewell at the airstrip.

“We left the NGO because we missed the smell of rain on the savannah, the weight of binoculars around our necks, and the look on a guest’s face when they see their first elephant at dawn. This is what we were born to do.” — Ken & Daisy, BobKen Tours
